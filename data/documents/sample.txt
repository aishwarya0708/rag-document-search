BOEING 737 MAINTENANCE MANUAL - WING ASSEMBLY

Section 5.2: Torque Specifications

Wing Bolt Assembly:
- Primary wing bolts: 45-50 ft-lbs
- Secondary fasteners: 25-30 ft-lbs
- Safety wire tension: 8-10 lbs

Section 5.3: Safety Protocols

All technicians must wear:
- Safety glasses
- Steel-toe boots
- High-visibility vest

Composite Material Handling:
- Use nitrile gloves at all times
- Store materials in climate-controlled environment (65-75°F)
- Maximum exposure time: 30 minutes outside controlled storage

Section 5.4: Compliance Requirements

FAA Documentation:
- All maintenance must be logged within 24 hours
- Supervisor sign-off required for critical systems
- Retain records for minimum 7 years
